What this means for a Bolt+ week
Even a short, 1-week Bolt+ promo delivers a double effect: (1) a sharp jump in orders from subscribers specifically during the promo week, well above the network's organic growth rate; (2) part of that effect persists after the discount ends (orders in the week after are still above the pre-promo baseline) — the promo doesn't just pull forward future orders, it builds a habit of coming back.
